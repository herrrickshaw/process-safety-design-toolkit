## Unreleased - 2026-11-07: dense-gas screening (Britter-McQuaid, far-field regime)

Equation exponent signs lost in the PDF text were fixed by the document's worked examples.
* Rejected: the EPA aerosol correction (5.4-2 to 5.4-6). Equation 5.4-2 was unreadable and an energy-balance reconstruction did not reproduce the document's example (zc 0.188), so the caller supplies an adjusted source state. `expansion` gives the flash fraction that feeds that adjustment.

## Unreleased - 2026-11-07: atmospheric expansion of orifice releases (ATEX) and flash fraction

Source: Witlox and Fernandez, IChemE Hazards 26 (Symposium Series 161), 2016, ATEX equations (1)-(10), read in full. Momentum and energy forms are derived from (1)-(3), not copied from the garbled extraction of (9)-(10).

Stagnation temperature and gamma were not stated, so 288 K and 1.27-1.31 were assumed; no number from this run is in the tests.

## Unreleased - 2026-11-07: Chamberlain vapour jet-flame geometry and surface emissive power
* New `process_safety/jet_flame.py` (library only, no CLI yet): conical-frustum vapour jet flame from the post-expansion state: effective source diameter, still-air length by solving Kalghatgi's correlation, flame length in wind and at a release angle, flame-axis angle, lift-off, frustum length, base and tip widths, surface emissive power with a cap. Source: DNV "Theory - Jet Fire" (JFSH), December 2023, section 2, equations (1)-(4), (15)-(18), (20), (21), (23), (25), (28), (31)-(33), (38), read in full from the public PDF; the Chamberlain and Kalghatgi originals were not read. Only equations are used; no DNV text is copied (the PDF carries a no-reproduction notice).
* Decisions: stdlib only and no property lookups, so density, velocity and heat of combustion are inputs (rejected: bundling CoolProp or a heat-of-combustion table). beta = 2.85, the paraffin W_st form and the 350 kW/m2 SEP cap are DNV's stated defaults and are overridable. Rejected: the Johnson horizontal-release correlations, the two-phase source diameter and the Miller low-luminosity model (DNV sections 3-4) in this change; the module says so in its notes and refuses nothing silently.
* Settled by checks, not assumed (the PDF text extraction is ambiguous): equation (18) uses 1726*sqrt(R - 0.026) + 134, the only reading continuous with the 8000 R branch at R = 0.05 (test); equation (25) uses xi(Ds)**C in the exponent. Both should be confirmed against Chamberlain (1987). Equation (18) can give a negative angle for horizontal releases in wind; it is clamped to 0 and a note is returned.
* Tests (`tests/test_jet_flame.py`, 16): identities only, namely velocity/radius consistency, Ds definition, the Kalghatgi residual, the momentum-dominated limit L_B0 W_st/(beta Ds) -> 0.2**-1.5, monotonic flow and wind trends, alpha continuity, the frustum triangle closing, radiated-power balance F_s m Hc = SEP x A, the cylinder limit of the area, the Cook F_s factors and the refusals.
* Plausibility check, NOT validation: the seven Chamberlain field trials whose inputs DNV tabulates (validation document Table 2) were run with an approximate natural-gas heat of combustion. Frustum lengths come out 17-54 m, inside the 12-52 m span of DNV's Figure 1 except Test 3D (54 m); trial-3 SEP 195-242 kW/m2 sits in the plotted measured range (about 185-255), but trial-4 SEP (245-347 kW/m2) is above it. DNV gives the measured values only as plots, so no per-test error was computed and no number was added to the tests. Next step: digitise the measurements or read Chamberlain's tables, then add a cross-check.
