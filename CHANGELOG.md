## Unreleased - 2026-11-10: delayed-equilibrium option in `line_flow` (fix 1 of the survey)
* `line_discharge(..., relaxation_length_m=None)`: vapour quality x_eq(1 - exp(-s/L_r)), s the distance since the pressure fell below the vapour pressure; the momentum equation gains the dv/ds term, the choke condition is unchanged.
