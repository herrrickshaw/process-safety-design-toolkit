# Changelog (decisions, including rejected alternatives)

## Unreleased - 2026-11-10: inverse analysis of the deviations above 10 %
* `tests/crosscheck/inverse_analysis.py` solves, one parameter at a time, for the value that would close each large deviation; results in `docs/INVERSE_ANALYSIS.md` and the `Inverse_Analysis` sheet of the final calculation workbook (live formulas where analytic). Findings: no single plausible parameter closes the flame-length gap against measurement (mass x0.23 to 0.59 means 80 to 92 % rain-out; velocity 440 to 460 m/s exceeds sound speed; angles and winds contradict the records), so it is structural; the SEP gap against DNV Phast is one constant factor of about 0.905 (model / DNV 1.09, 1.11, 1.10) in an unidentified quantity (eq 38 and Cook Fs match DNV's printed equations), to be settled with DNV's intermediate values; JIVE 8051 closes with an effective discharge pressure of 1.62 bar abs (or fittings K 73); AA 3029 needs +10 % vessel pressure; droplet C_ds needs 11.8 at 5 bar and 22.9 at 11 bar, so no single value; DNV's propane post-expansion velocities imply 11.5 and 12.1 bar abs exit pressures, an artefact of its tuned upstream pressure.
* Not adopted: none of the fit values changes a default. Rejected: tuning flame length with a rain-out fraction, velocity or wind that the records contradict; calibrating the radiant fraction to the Project AA mean SEP (Shell's own models give lower fractions).

## Unreleased - 2026-11-10: compressibility Z for fire-case relief determined from API 520 Part I
* Read API 520 Part I (supplied copy; clauses 5.6.1, 5.6.2.4, 5.6.3.2.2, Annex B) and reproduced its Annex B real-gas isentropic flux with CoolProp (`tests/crosscheck/check_real_gas_flux.py`). * Rejected: using the calculated saturated-vapour Z in the API equation (unsafe and outside 5.6.1); using the real-gas flux as the only basis (needs an equation of state the toolkit does not have).
