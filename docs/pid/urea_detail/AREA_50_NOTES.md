## Physical-solvent concept (all A, no vendor data)
The Rev B repo module `acid_gas_removal.py` is a mass-balance split with a Kremser screening note and carries no solvent properties, so everything below is a generic physical-solvent screening: feed CO2 partial pressure 19.0 bar (D); rich loading 34.2 g CO2/kg = 0.60 x 3.0 g/kg/bar x pCO2 (A: approach 0.5-0.8, coefficient 2-5 g/kg/bar); 6 % of the CO2 flashed at 18 bar and recycled (A 3-10 %). Results (D): lean circulation 9,939 t/h; liquid load limit 100 m3/m2/h (A 50-150) requires 6 shells of 4.6 m (max 5 m, A); absorption heat 32.1 MW, rich solvent 10.8 C; P-301 19.7 MW (50 bar, eff 0.70 A); chiller 26.1 MW; NH3 refrigeration 8.0 MW at 55 % of Carnot (A), NH3 circulation 68.7 t/h. Only 10 % of the flashed solvent (A 5-30 %) is hot-stripped in C-302 (reboiler 27.0 MW with a stripping ratio of 10 kg/kg H2S, A 5-20; E-301 54.3 MW at effectiveness 0.90).
Stream 10 is released entirely by flashing at 2 bar (Rev B pressure) at 5 C; Rev B lists 40 C and the cold-recovery exchanger is not drawn. D-301 removes the water the CO2 would carry (0.2 % of the CO2, saturation at 5 C) before K-601; dry CO2 leaves at 20 C, 1.8 bar (A).
FINDINGS: (1) bulk lean solvent keeps about 3,000 ppm (mass) of H2S because only 10 % is regenerated, so the Rev B 99.9 % H2S removal is not demonstrated; (2) Rev B stream 11 is pure H2S while a real acid gas carries co-stripped CO2 (typically 20-50 % H2S, A): the sulphur unit is a lower bound on gas volume; (3) co-absorbed H2/CO is not quantified (needs solubility data), the HP flash recycle is drawn as pure CO2.

## Methanation and purification
Oxides in stream 12 are 3.9 mol %; heat of reaction 19.9 MW (206 / 165 kJ/mol, A); one adiabatic bed would rise 241 K, so two beds with interbed cooler E-305 (9.5 MW) and a feed/effluent exchanger E-304 (19.9 MW) are drawn. FINDING: this is far above the normal methanator duty; deeper CO2 removal or a nitrogen wash / PSA (Rev B ISD 3) is the better route. Decision on N2 wash: not drawn, because the Rev B balance supplies loop N2 from ASU stream 14 at K-401; PU-301 is a concept box that splits H2 (to K-401) from stream 13 and assumes 100 % H2 recovery (a PSA would lose about 10-15 %, A, not computed). G-301 is a sulphur guard for the nickel catalyst (Rev B leaves 0.03 mol/s H2S in stream 12).

## Sulphur recovery (Claus, generic, derived by stoichiometry)
H2S 27.4 mol/s (stream 11) plus 1.2 mol/s recycled from the tail-gas absorber; air for one third of the H2S to SO2 (H2S + 1.5 O2 to SO2 + H2O), 2 H2S + SO2 to 3 S + 2 H2O. Extents (A): thermal 0.65 (0.55-0.70), converter 1 0.70 (0.60-0.80), converter 2 0.60 (0.50-0.70), giving 95.8 % Claus recovery; tail-gas hydrogenation (H2 1.2 x stoich, A) and absorber capture 99.5 % (A 99-99.9) give 99.98 % overall. Sulphur product 3.16 t/h = 76 t/d (D). Temperatures A: furnace 1,150 C, WHB out 300 C, condensers 170 / 170 / 130 C, converter inlets 230 / 210 C, hydrogenation 280 C. Equilibrium limits are not computed, so the recovery is optimistic if equilibrium governs. Claus heat 100 kJ/mol SO2 (A 80-150), hydrogenation 207 kJ/mol (A 180-230), sulphur latent heat 300 kJ/kg (A 280-320). Pure-H2S feed with air gives a very hot flame: split flow or oxygen moderation is a licensor choice (gap). Stack SO2 limit, dispersion and height are not sourced.
