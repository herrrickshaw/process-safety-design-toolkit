## What this changes for the capacity shortlist

- Urea: two PIB-sourced approved 12.7 LMTPA projects (Talcher coal-gas, Namrup) give a verified-by-two-releases base of 1.27 Mt/yr.
- CBG: PIB supports 8 TPD (fleet mean) to 14 TPD (HPCL Budaun) output; feed 100 TPD for the flagship.
- Refinery, LPG bottling, ethanol, biodiesel, SAF, LNG liquefaction, fruit and veg: PIB gives only context. Plant-level nameplates come from the three analogue reports.
- Conflicts to resolve before use: urea national capacity (283.74 vs 269.42 LMTPA), LNG terminal totals (47.7 vs 52.7 MMTPA).
