## Unit systems

CLI scenarios marked si_us were run twice: once in SI and once with every dimensioned number converted to US-customary units and passed through `--units us`. Python-API, JSON-tool and text-output scenarios are SI only; their inputs have no unit-bearing command-line flags, so `python -m process_safety convert` is how you prepare US values for them.
